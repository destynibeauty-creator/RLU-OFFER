Why it doesn't last.

Act One

Your adhesive is not drying.
It is curing.

Cyanoacrylate does not dry like paint. It is a chemical reaction that uses moisture in the air to set. That is why your room is part of your kit.

Act One

Faster and stronger is not better.

More cyanoacrylate means a faster bond and less room for error. It is also the most common cause of client allergy, and sensitization is delayed and cumulative. A client who has been fine for years can swell after a fill.

That goes for you too, breathing it over every set.

Act One · The Cure Check
  • Too dry. Under-cures. Stays tacky. Slides off in a few days.
  • In range. Strong, flexible bond.
  • Too humid, too fast. Shock-cures. Blooms. Goes brittle.

There is no universal humidity rule.

40 to 70 percent. 70 to 72 degrees. Whoever taught you that taught you somebody else's adhesive. Those numbers are product-dependent.

Act One

Two artists. Same glue.
Different retention.

Different rooms. Different seasons. Get a hygrometer, learn your adhesive's range, and read your own air.

Act One

She swore she wasn't using oil.
She was telling the truth.

The glands along her lid margin keep her lash line moist. That same oil is dissolving your bond, a little every day, whether she touches a product or not.

An oily client sheds faster
no matter how clean your application was.

That is not you failing. That is her chemistry, and now you can explain it instead of apologising for it.

Act Three · Aftercare
  • Keep the lash line clean. Brush. Foam cleanse.
  • No oil-based products near the eyes.
  • Do not rub, tug or pull. No mascara.
  • Water timing per your adhesive, not a blanket rule.

"No water for 24 hours"
is not a rule. It's a rumor.

Give her the timing your manufacturer specifies. Anything else is you repeating what somebody told you.

The part most artists skip

Tell her why.

"I'm cleansing first so the adhesive bonds to a clean lash. That's half your retention right there."

A client who understands the why protects it at home. A client who was just handed a list does not.
