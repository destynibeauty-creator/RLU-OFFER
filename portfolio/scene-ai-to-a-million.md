# THE SCENE AI: what $1M actually looks like
August 25, 2026. Arithmetic, not opinion.

---

## First, $9 a month cannot do it

$1,000,000 over twelve months, with members growing steadily, needs about
**18,500 members at month twelve.**

From eight. In a year. That is not a stretch goal, it is a different universe.
Skool's largest communities live in the low thousands.

**So the ladder is not optional. It is the whole business.** Every plan below
assumes that.

---

One operator. A channel that works. Everything running hard.

| Line | 12-month ceiling | What it costs her |
|---|---|---|
| Membership, $9/mo, 2,000 by month 12 | $108,000 | The channel |
| $97 unlock, 20% of members | $19,400 | Nothing extra |

Three hundred thousand. That is the ceiling of what is currently built, run at
full tilt, by one person.

It is a real business. It is not a million.

## The piece the ladder is missing

There is no cohort in it.

$9 a month, then $97, then done-for-you, then a retainer. Everything above $97
is delivery work, and delivery work does not scale past her hours.

**A live cohort is the only product on earth that sells at four figures and
delivers to sixty people at once.**

| | |
|---|---|
| 3 cohorts a year × 60 seats × $1,997 | **$359,460** |
| Added to the ladder above | **$663,740** |

**And she already knows how to run one.** That is the whole point. Rich Lash
University is a live cohort business that sells at $2,997. She has run it,
filled it, taught it, graduated it. The single most valuable thing RLU owns is
not the lash content, it is that she knows how to hold sixty women through six
weeks and get paid for it.

Nobody in the AI-character space is doing that. They sell templates and Discord
servers. She could sell a cohort.

**That is the highest-leverage move available to her in either business, and it
is currently in neither plan.**

## The gap to a million

$663,740 with everything above working. **$336,260 short.**

Three ways to close it, and she probably needs two of them.

**1. Reprice the core.** $9 is the binding constraint on the whole model.

| Core price | Members needed at month 12 |
|---|---|
| $9 | 18,519 |
| $47 | 3,546 |
| $97 | 1,718 |

**Her own rule permits this.** The rule says $9 flat until 50,000 members, but
the founding promise is that the price locks *for the member*, and the only
permitted scarcity line is "the price goes up as we grow." Grandfather the
founders forever, raise it for everyone after. That is not breaking the rule,
it is the rule working as written.

**2. Take DFY off her hands.** Fifteen premieres a month is fifteen hours a
week she will not have. Either cap DFY at five a month as a premium halo, or
train someone to produce them. It cannot be both high volume and her.

**3. Run four cohorts instead of three.** $120k more, and the fourth is the
easiest because the machine is already built.

## So can she do it

**In twelve months: $400k to $700k is the honest range if everything works.**

**$1M is a month 18 to 24 number** on this structure, unless she hires.

That is not a no. That is a timeline, and it comes from one specific fact:
**the first ninety days are not revenue, they are finding the channel.** If she
cracks acquisition by November, she has nine or ten selling months, and $1M in
ten months means $100k a month by mid-year. That is a business with
infrastructure, not a solo operator with a Skool group.

**What would have to be true for twelve:**

1. A repeatable acquisition channel, proven, inside 90 days
2. Core repriced to $47 or above for new members
3. A cohort at $1,997 or more, run three or four times
4. DFY capped or delegated
5. **SCENE AI becomes the main business and RLU becomes the other one**

Number five is the real price, and it should be a decision, not a drift.

## The correction that reprices this whole document

**Seven of the eight members are friends and family. One is a stranger.**

Every projection above multiplies an acquisition rate, and the measured rate
is **one stranger out of 7,364 unique viewers in thirty days.** Not eight.

That is not failure at fifteen days. Most launches convert zero. But it means
this document is a model of a business that does not have a funnel yet, and
none of its numbers should be planned against until one exists.

**Meanwhile RLU's 16 Campus members are all strangers, and one of them bought
at $2,997.** The mechanism that produced them is ads, then a live event where
she teaches, then the offer. THE SCENE AI has no live event.

**That is the gap. Not the price, not the ladder, not the partner network.**
Everything on this page waits behind a free live hour that does not exist yet.

## The good news, and it is genuinely good

**33,982 views in thirty days on a brand that was two weeks old.**

Attention is the hardest input in this entire model and she already has it.
Most people building a community have to buy that or wait years for it. She
generated it, organically, while building the thing.

**What she does not have is conversion.** Zero net followers on 34,000 views,
and eight members with no confirmed source for any of them.

That is a much better problem than the other way around. Traffic without
conversion is a plumbing problem. Conversion without traffic is a life
sentence.

## The next ninety days, and there is only one job

**Find out where member number nine comes from.**

Not the ladder. Not the retainer. Not the partner network. One question:
**what reliably turns a viewer into a member.**

Until that is answered, every number above is fiction, because all of them
multiply the same unknown.

**Three things to run, in this order:**

**1. Test "Comment SCENE" end to end this week.** It is documented as built and
never confirmed working. Five minutes. If the automation is broken, that alone
may explain 34,000 views and eight members.

**2. Put a join CTA on every single post for thirty days.** The working theory
in the handoff is that the content never asked. That theory is marked as an
assumption and it has never been tested. Test it.

**3. Track the source of every member from today.** One line in a note. Where
they came from, what they saw. Twenty entries and she will know more about her
business than any plan I could write.

**Then on October 28, with real attribution and a finished RLU cohort, the $1M
question stops being a hope and becomes a calculation.** Cost per member times
the number she needs equals the budget. That is a plan. What she has today is
a target.
