01  The Cure Check

Your adhesive is not drying, it is curing, and it pulls moisture out of the air to do it. Too dry it under-cures and sheds. Too humid it goes brittle. Get a hygrometer and read your own room.

02  The Four Questions

What is she putting on her face at night. How the shower hits her face. Is she wearing mascara. When was her last full set. Ask all four before you touch her, then say out loud what you heard.

03  The Protocol

Cleanse, prime, bond, cure. In that order, every single set. Then on day three you text her: wanted to make sure your lashes are feeling good.
