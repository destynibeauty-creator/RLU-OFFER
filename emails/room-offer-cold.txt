01  THE CURE CHECK
Your adhesive is not drying, it is curing, and it pulls moisture out of the
air to do it. Too dry it under-cures and sheds. Too humid it goes brittle.
Get a hygrometer and read your own room.

02  THE FOUR QUESTIONS
What is she putting on her face at night. How the shower hits her face. Is
she wearing mascara. When was her last full set. Ask all four before you
touch her, then say out loud what you heard.

03  THE PROTOCOL
Cleanse, prime, bond, cure. In that order, every single set. Then on day
three you text her: wanted to make sure your lashes are feeling good.
