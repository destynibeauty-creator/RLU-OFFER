I posted lashes for nine years.
This did 2.6 million.

Sunday I posted the first episode of my story. Not a set. Not a before and after. Not a tip.

Me. My life. The part I usually skip.

2.6 million views in three days. On TikTok alone, over eleven thousand hours of people watching it.

I have posted hundreds of lash sets over nine years. Beautiful work. Not one of them ever did a fraction of what my story did in one night.

Here is why that matters to you.

Your feed is full of lashes. So is everybody else's. A woman scrolling cannot tell your set apart from the girl three cities over, and she is not going to try.

But there is only one of you.

She is not booking a lash set. She is booking a person she decided she trusts, and she decides that before she ever really looks at your work.

"My work is not good enough yet for me to be talking about myself."

I thought that for years. It is backwards. Nobody follows you because your fans are perfect. They follow you because they know something about you.

So this week, one post. Not a set.

Why you started. What you were doing before this. What was happening in your life the day you decided to learn lashes.

Tell it like a story, not a caption. A beginning, a middle, and the part you would rather leave out.
