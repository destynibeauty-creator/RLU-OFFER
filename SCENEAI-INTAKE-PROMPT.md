# Prompt to run in the SCENE AI assistant

Copy everything below the line and paste it in. Bring the answer back here.

---

I need you to write a factual handoff document about THE SCENE AI. It is going
to another assistant that manages my other business, Rich Lash® University, so
we can work out how I split my time between the two. It has never heard of
THE SCENE AI.

**Rules for this document, and they matter more than completeness:**

- Facts only. No pitch, no encouragement, no "you've built something
  special."
- **If you do not know something, write UNKNOWN.** Do not estimate, do not
  infer, do not fill a gap with something plausible. A made up number will get
  planned around and it will cost me real money.
- Mark anything that is my opinion or a guess as [ASSUMPTION].
- Where you have a real number, give the number and say where it came from.
- Output it as one plain markdown document I can copy whole. Not a
  conversation, not a summary, not bullet points that trail off.

**Cover these ten things, in this order:**

**1. The brand.** Name, what it actually is in one sentence, who it is for,
every URL and handle, follower counts, when it started.

**2. What is sold.** Every offer with its price, what is included, and whether
it is recurring or one time. Say plainly which of these exist today and which
are still an idea.

**3. Members and money.** How many paying members right now. What each pays.
Monthly recurring total. Total revenue to date if known. Any refunds, churn or
cancellations so far.

**4. Costs.** What it costs me to run monthly. Tools, subscriptions, ad spend,
anything I pay for that only exists because of this business.

**5. Where the members came from.** Every one of them if you know. Organic
post, ad, DM, referral, someone I already knew. This is the most important
section in the document, so be specific and say UNKNOWN where you are.

**6. Time.** What I am committed to weekly or monthly. Live calls, posts,
support, anything with a date on it. Roughly how many hours a week this
currently takes. If you do not know, say so and I will answer it myself.

**7. What has been tried.** What worked, what did not, and any numbers
attached. Content that performed, offers that flopped, ads that ran.

**8. Brand rules.** Voice, tone, hex codes, fonts, banned words, anything
where I have said "never do this."

**9. What exists.** Assets I already have. Pages, funnels, email lists and
their sizes, content library, community structure, templates, automations.

**10. What is next.** Anything already scheduled or promised to members, with
dates. Then, separately and clearly labelled as my intention rather than a
fact: what I have said I want this business to become.

**One more thing at the end.** List every question in this document you could
not answer, so I know exactly what I still have to fill in myself.
