# How Destyni talks about retention

Recorded August 24, 2026, in her words. This governs every asset.

## Do not use

| Word | Why |
|---|---|
| hold, holds, held, "will it hold" | Not her vocabulary. |
| fail, fails, failed, "sets fail" | Same. See the note below on "retention fails". |
| didn't hold, did not hold | Named directly by her. |
| let go, lets go | Named directly by her. |

## Use instead

Lash shedding. Shedding. Shedding fast. Premature shedding.

Falling out early. Falling out too soon. Falling out too quick.
Extensions falling out. Premature fallout.

Poor retention. Bad retention. Bonding issues. Retention fails.

Didn't last.

## Two notes

**"Retention fails" is on her list even though "fail" is off it.** The verb is
the problem, not the noun. "Why sets fail" is wrong. "Retention fails" as a
thing that happens is hers.

**"Fall off" is not on her list, "falling out" is.** She has not banned "fall
off" and the hero hook still uses it: "Why her lashes fall off in nine days."
That line is also the ad hook and the email subject, so it is not being
changed without her. If she wants alignment, the swap is "fall out".
