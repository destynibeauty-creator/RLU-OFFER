## The strategic model

The Campus is a holding pen, not a profit centre. Alexandria sat in it for 12
to 18 months, was never pitched, then bought Equipped at $2,997. The Campus is
the only mechanism that has produced an organic full price buyer, so the job
is to grow it and wait.

The Retention Room feeds the Campus. The Campus feeds the certification.

**Positioning:** everyone in the category teaches technique. She is the only
Registered Nurse in it, and she teaches the explanation. "Certified but not
confident" is the number one objection in lash education, and it exists
because skills-only training does not produce confidence.

## Brand rules, non negotiable

**Never use:** em dashes, a sentence starting with "And", brown in any asset,
the words free, students, tuition. Banned: scale, elevate, unlock, hustle,
passive income, six figures, boss babe, fully booked, dream clients, leverage.

**Never say hold or fail** about retention. She says shedding, premature
shedding, falling out too soon, poor retention, bonding issues, didn't last.

**Never make a forward looking income or earnings promise, in any form.**
Documented past results may be shown only if real, screenshot backed, labelled
as whose they are, and sat beside a plain results-vary line.
