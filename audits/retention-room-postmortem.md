## What I would change about the offer email

I told you to lead with the certification and keep the $9 quiet so it would
not cannibalize. For the 34 warm women that still holds. For the other 135
it is wrong, and I did not have this data when I said it.

Cannibalization only matters if the certification was winnable. On a list
that is nine days old with one buyer in it, it mostly was not. The $149 kit
and the $9 campus are the realistic conversions there, and a first purchase
of any size is what turns a stranger into someone who buys the
certification in November.

**Send two versions.**

- **Tiers 1 to 3 (34 women):** the email as built. Certification first,
  pink button, kit and campus underneath.
- **Tiers 4 and 5 (135 women):** same email, flipped. The $149 kit gets the
  button, the campus sits next to it, the certification moves to the bottom
  as a line that says where this goes if she wants it. No deadline
  pressure. She met you last week.

## For the next one

1. **Stop selling the certification on night one to a cold room.** Sell the
   kit or the campus, then sell the certification to the buyers 30 to 60
   days later. That is the pattern your own best year already proved.
2. **Turn on Zoom registration** so attendance ties to an email address
   instead of a list of iPhones.
3. **Move the turn earlier.** You promised one hour and the offer landed
   around 8:50. For a stated 60 minutes the turn belongs at minute 33 to
   35.
4. **Segment the invite by list age.** A woman who joined 5 days ago and a
   woman who has been reading you for a year should not get the same
   reminder sequence, and right now they do.
