# Canva Brand Kit Update: "Rich Lash Tech"

The kit Canva is pulling from is stale. It does not hold Poppins, so every design
Canva generates comes out in a heavy condensed font that is not RLU. Fix the kit
once and every future generation lands closer on the first try.

Canva > Brand > Brand Kits > **Rich Lash Tech** > Edit

---

## 1. Fonts

Canva Pro lets you upload font files. Get Poppins from Google Fonts
(fonts.google.com/specimen/Poppins > Get font > Download all), unzip, then upload
these four files under **Brand fonts**:

| Role | File | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Headings | `Poppins-ExtraLight.ttf` (200) | Big display type, section headlines |
| Subheadings | `Poppins-Light.ttf` (300) | Sublines, body copy |
| Body | `Poppins-Regular.ttf` (400) | Long paragraphs |
| Accent | `Poppins-SemiBold.ttf` (600) | Buttons, eyebrows, all-caps labels |

Also upload `Poppins-LightItalic.ttf` (300 italic). The italic is doing real work
on the live page: every emphasized word in a headline is italic Poppins Light in
pink. Without it Canva fakes the slant and it looks wrong.

**Delete or demote whatever font is currently set as the heading font.** As long
as it sits in the Heading slot, Canva keeps reaching for it.

## 2. Colors

Replace the whole palette with these. Nothing else belongs in the kit.

| Swatch | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Black | `#080808` | Every background. Not #000000. |
| Off-black | `#0E0C0E` | Cards and panels sitting on black |
| Ink | `#F2EEF0` | Primary text. Not pure white. |
| Soft | `#A39BA0` | Secondary text, sublines |
| Faint | `#6A626A` | Fine print, disclaimers |
| Hot pink | `#F600A2` | Brand pink. Buttons, italic emphasis. |
| Pink soft | `#FF5CC5` | Pink text on black (the flat pink is too dark to read small) |
| Gold | `#D4AF37` | Detail lines, borders |
| Gold soft | `#E8C57A` | Gold text on black |
| Green | `#4EBB19` | Confirmations, guarantee marks |
| Green soft | `#7EE84D` | Green text on black |

**Remove any brown, tan, taupe, or beige swatch.** Brown is a brand rule violation
and if it is in the kit Canva will use it.

---

## 3. Layout rules the kit cannot enforce

Canva will keep defaulting to these, so correct them by hand every time:

- **Left-align everything.** Canva centers by default. RLU does not center.
- **No decorative lines, no divider strokes, no icon shapes.** Canva adds them
  unprompted. Delete them.
- **Type sits bottom-left.** Photo or negative space fills the top two-thirds.
- **Margin is 80px** on a 1080-wide canvas.

## 4. Words Canva keeps producing that are banned

Canva's AI wrote "Elevate Your Business" and "Unlock Your Potential" on the first
pass of the Lash Cash cover, unprompted. Watch for and delete:

scale · elevate · unlock · hustle · passive income · six figures · boss babe ·
fully booked · dream clients · leverage · free · students · tuition

Also delete any sentence Canva writes that promises earnings, in any form. That is
the one rule with legal teeth behind it.
