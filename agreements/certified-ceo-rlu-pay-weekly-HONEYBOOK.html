3. RLU Pay™ Terms: Autopay, Missed Payments, and Communication

Autopay is required.

After your deposit you receive your RLU Pay™ invoice for the six weekly payments in Section 2. To activate the plan you must enter a valid payment method and enroll in autopay on that invoice. By doing so you authorize RLU to charge that payment method automatically for each scheduled payment on its due date until your balance is paid in full, and you agree to keep a valid payment method on file for the length of the plan.

Reach out before the date, not after.

If a payment is going to fail, or you need to move a date, email learn@richlash.university before the due date. RLU Pay™ runs on communication and we will work with you when you tell us in advance. This is the whole reason the plan exists without a credit check.

What happens if a payment is missed.

These steps apply automatically and do not require separate notice, though we will always try to reach you first.

  • 1 to 2 days past due: your payment method is retried. No penalty.
  • 3 days past due: a $35 late fee is added to your balance, and access to the portal, the live sessions, and the community is suspended until your account is current.
  • 15 days past due: the entire remaining balance becomes immediately due in full and the weekly schedule ends.
  • 30 days past due: your enrollment may be terminated and your account referred for collection.

Your certification is released when your balance is paid.

RLU will not issue your Master Eyelash Specialist Certification, your Barbicide® credential, or any letter or record of completion while a balance is outstanding. Completing the coursework does not release the credential. Paying in full does.

Suspension does not pause your schedule.
