### 3. RLU Pay™ Terms: Autopay, Missed Payments, and Communication

**Autopay is required.** After your deposit you receive your RLU Pay™ invoice for the six
weekly payments in Section 2. To activate the plan you must enter a valid payment method and
enroll in autopay on that invoice. By doing so you authorize RLU to charge that payment
method automatically for each scheduled payment on its due date until your balance is paid
in full, and you agree to keep a valid payment method on file for the length of the plan.

**Reach out before the date, not after.** If a payment is going to fail, or you need to move
a date, email learn@richlash.university **before the due date.** RLU Pay™ runs on
communication and we will work with you when you tell us in advance. This is the whole
reason the plan exists without a credit check.

**What happens if a payment is missed.** These steps apply automatically and do not require
separate notice, though we will always try to reach you first.

| Days past due | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | Your payment method is retried. No penalty. |
| 3 | A **$35 late fee** is added to your balance. Access to the portal, the live sessions, and the community is **suspended** until your account is current. |
| 15 | The **entire remaining balance becomes immediately due in full**, and the weekly schedule ends. |
| 30 | Your enrollment may be terminated and your account referred for collection. |

**Your certification is released when your balance is paid.** RLU will not issue your Master
Eyelash Specialist Certification, your Barbicide® credential, or any letter or record of
completion while a balance is outstanding. Completing the coursework does not release the
credential. Paying in full does.

**Your kit.** Your Money Maker Lash Kit ships before Week 1 in reliance on your full payment
plan. It is not a separate purchase and no part of your deposit is allocated to it. If your
enrollment is terminated for non payment, you remain responsible for the full balance and
you may be invoiced separately for the kit at its replacement cost.

**Suspension does not pause your schedule.** Payment dates continue on the schedule in
Section 2 whether or not you attend, participate, or have access.

**Collection costs.** If your account is referred for collection, you are responsible for
reasonable collection costs and attorney's fees actually incurred by RLU, to the extent
permitted by Georgia law.
