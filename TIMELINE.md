## Two dates that are easy to confuse

**2019 is when she started teaching. 2023 is when she took it online.**
Earlier notes recorded 2023 as the start of teaching. That was wrong, and it
undersold her by four years.

## Why the distinction matters

**250+ women trained over seven years is about 36 a year.** That is a
believable pace for a solo educator who is also lashing and also nursing.
Compressed into three years it would be 83 a year, which reads as inflated
to anyone doing the arithmetic. The real timeline makes the real number
credible.

When the store shows three orders in twelve months, that is a two-year-old
