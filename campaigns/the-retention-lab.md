# THE RETENTION LAB
An eight day campaign for The Retention Room. Mon Aug 24 to Tue Sept 1.

---

## The idea

Everybody in the lash space posts finished sets. Pretty work, good lighting,
a trending sound. It all looks the same because it is the same.

**Nobody has ever shown what is actually happening at the bond.**

You cannot film it. The bond point between a natural lash and an extension is
too small, the cure happens in two seconds, and the failure happens over nine
days inside somebody's shower. It is the single most important thing in the
entire craft and it has never been seen.

AI can render it. And you are the only person in this space with the standing
to show it, because you are the nurse.

**The campaign is one sentence: see what is actually happening.**

That is why it works for all three of your goals at once. Organic saves and
shares it because it is genuinely new. Ads convert on it because it is the
mechanism, not a promise. And Campus sells itself on it, because once she
believes retention is knowable, she wants to be in the room where you know it.

---

## The visual system

Everything in the campaign uses the same four things so eight days of posts
read as one campaign and not eight ideas.

**Ground:** #0A0A0A. Never white, never beige, never brown.

**Data pink:** #F600A2 for the live readout, the gauge fill, the numbers.

**Gold:** #D4AF37 for labels, frames, the RLU mark.

**One recurring object:** the gauge row. 72°F, 55%, 2 to 3s. It is already
made. It appears in almost every piece. By day eight, people recognise it
before they read it. That is what a campaign is.

## The four formats

### 1. THE MACRO. The showstopper.

Impossible footage. What the eye cannot see.

Three to shoot:

**a. The cure.** Extreme macro on a single adhesive droplet at the bond point.
Moisture in the air reaching it. The surface hardening from the outside in.
Two to three seconds of real time stretched to fifteen.

**b. The nine days.** Same bond. Time compressing. Oil creeping along the lash
shaft, reaching the bond, the polymer clouding, softening, releasing. The
extension slides off. No lash was touched, nothing was pulled. It let go on
its own.

**c. The humidity fork.** Split screen. Same droplet, two rooms. One at 30%,
one at 55%. One cures slow and stays soft. One sets clean. Gauges running in
the corner the whole time.

Caption pattern, all three: one line of what you just saw, one line of what it
means, one line to the Room.

> That is nine days in nine seconds.
> Nobody pulled anything. The bond dissolved.
> Tuesday, 8 PM ET, I am showing you how to stop it. Link in bio.

### 2. THE AUTOPSY. The workhorse.

Recurring format. Same open, same layout, every time. This is the one that
becomes yours.

A set photo. A HUD panel over it: adhesive, room temp, humidity, days lasted.
Then your voice over the top, thirty seconds, naming the cause.

> Case eleven. Nine days. She said she wasn't using oil.
> Look at the base. See how the fan is closed at the bottom?
> That is not oil. That is a cure that never finished.

Three of these in eight days. Number them from the start, because a numbered
series makes people want the next one and the back catalogue.

**Use your own sets or ones you have permission for.** Never a stranger's work
off the internet. This format only has power because it is honest.

### 3. YOUR FACE. Non negotiable.

**AI is the set design. It is not the actor.**

Your highest performing content is you, in a real room, talking. 23.3K on
"I didn't leave nursing to chase lashes." That is the number to respect.

At least three of the eight days are you, filmed on your phone, no overlay, no
generated anything. The AI pieces earn the scroll. Your face earns the trust.
A feed of only generated content in 2026 reads as a person with nothing real
to show, and that is the opposite of the argument you are making.

### 4. THE ROOM. Environment b-roll.

Slow push through the studio render. Signage, then the gauge wall, then the
seats. Six seconds, no text, one line of caption. Use it twice, once to open
the campaign and once the night before.

Label it as generated in the caption every time. Costs nothing, protects the
screenshots.

## Generation prompts

Run these in whatever tool you are using. Every one of them carries the
palette line, because that is what got the bronze out of the studio render.

**The cure**
> Extreme macro photography, a single droplet of clear cyanoacrylate adhesive
> at the join between two dark fibres, shallow depth of field, the droplet
> surface hardening and crystallising from the outside inward, faint moisture
> in the air catching light, slow motion, scientific and clinical. Palette:
> near black #0A0A0A ground, hot pink #F600A2 rim light, gold #D4AF37 specular
> highlights. No brown, no beige, no warm wood. No text.

**The nine days**
> Extreme macro, a bond between two dark fibres, time lapse. A film of clear
> oil creeping slowly along the fibre and reaching the bond. The clear polymer
> clouding, softening, losing its grip. The upper fibre separating and sliding
> away on its own with nothing touching it. Clinical, slow, inevitable.
> Palette: near black #0A0A0A ground, hot pink #F600A2 accent light, gold
> #D4AF37 highlights. No brown, no beige. No text.

**The humidity fork**
> Split screen macro, identical adhesive droplets in two environments. Left
> side dry and still, the droplet staying wet and glossy. Right side with
> visible moisture in the air, the droplet setting to a clean matte finish.
> Clinical laboratory feel. Palette: near black #0A0A0A, hot pink #F600A2 data
> accents, gold #D4AF37. No brown. No text.

**Autopsy card frame**
> Dark clinical data card on near black, thin gold hairline frame, corner
> brackets, a large empty rectangle in the upper two thirds for a photograph,
> four small labelled data fields below it, hot pink accent line. Empty
> template, no words. Palette: #0A0A0A, #F600A2, #D4AF37.

**Room push**
> Slow forward camera push through a dark luxury lash training studio, gold
> and pink neon signage, a wall of data screens, workstations. Cinematic, six

> grey stone. No brown, no beige, no warm wood.

## Where it all points

**Organic.** Every caption ends the same way. One line, the date, the link in
bio. Not a paragraph. The content does the work, the caption just opens the
door.

**Ads.** The macro pieces are your best creative and you have not tested them.
If **THE NINE DAYS** performs organically by Friday, put it in the ad set for
the weekend. That is a real ad, made from a post that already proved it can
hold attention.

**Campus.** Only two pieces sell it directly, Saturday and September 2. Every
other piece sells the Room, and the Room sells the Campus at nine o'clock.
Selling $9 in every post makes the whole campaign feel like a funnel. Selling
it twice makes it feel like an invitation.

## The rules this follows

No income or earnings claim in any piece.

Only confirmed numbers: 250+ women trained, 1,000+ clients, nine years, RN
since 2020.

Her retention vocabulary. Shedding, falling out too soon, didn't last. Never
hold, never "sets fail". See BRAND-VOICE.md.

No brown, in any prompt, ever.

Every generated piece labelled as generated in its caption.
