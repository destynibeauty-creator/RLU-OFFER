# The always-on Campus ad
Middle of funnel. Warm traffic to the $9 Campus. Set once, leave running.

---

## Start it September 2, not today

This week every warm person should be going to the Room, which costs them
nothing and converts far better than a $9 ask. Then the Room sells the Campus
at nine o'clock, which is what it was built to do.

Running a $9 ad at the same audience you are inviting to a live night is
competing with yourself, and the paid one loses.

**September 2 is also when the pool is biggest.** Everyone who registered,
everyone who watched, everyone who hit the page and left. That is the day this
ad has the most people to talk to.

---

## Does it pay for itself

Not on the subscription alone. On the subscription plus what the pool
produces.

| | |
|---|---|
| Subscription | $9/mo, call it $108 a year before churn |
| Certification value | ~2% of paying members a year at ~$2,000 net ≈ **$40/member/year** |
| **Worth per member, year one** | **roughly $60 to $140** |

That supports a cost per member of **$30 to $40** comfortably.

**The 2% is an assumption, not a measurement.** It comes from Alexandria: one
conversion from about 85 free members over 18 months, which is 0.8% a year,
doubled on the reasoning that people who pay something convert better than
people who pay nothing. It is n=1. Treat $30 to $40 as a ceiling to test
against, not a fact.

**The real argument is not the subscription.** The Campus is a holding pen. It
is the only place a woman can stay in your world for a year until she is ready
for $2,997, and Alexandria is the proof it works. This ad is that mechanism,
running on its own.

## The audience

**Include, any of these:**

- Everyone who visited richlash.university in the last 180 days
- Everyone who engaged with your Instagram in the last 365 days
- Everyone who watched 50% or more of any of your videos, last 365 days
- Your Klaviyo list, uploaded as a custom audience

**Exclude, and this one matters:**

- **Current Campus members.** Export the list from Skool, upload it as a custom
  audience, exclude it. Otherwise you are paying to advertise $9 a month to
  women who already pay you $9 a month.
- Anyone who visited the Skool about page in the last 7 days. They just saw
  it. Give them a week before you show them again.

Refresh both exclusion uploads monthly. A stale exclusion list is money.

## Settings

| | |
|---|---|
| Budget | **$10 a day.** Not more. |
| Objective | Traffic, optimise for **Landing Page Views** |
| Placement | Advantage+, let it find them |
| Frequency | Check weekly. Over **3 in 7 days** means the pool is too small, drop the budget |
| Destination | The Skool about page, direct |

**$10 a day is deliberate.** Your retargeting pool is small. A small pool with
a big budget means the same women see the same ad six times a week and start
resenting you. Low and forever beats high and burnt.

---

## The problem you need to know about

**Skool is not your domain, so your pixel is almost certainly not on it.** That
means Meta cannot see a signup. You will not get a conversion number, a cost
per member, or an optimised delivery toward buyers.

**Do not solve it by adding a middle page.** Sending them to a Shopify page
that links to Skool gets you tracking and costs you a click, and at $10 a day
the click is worth more than the precision.

**Solve it by counting.** Write down your member count the day you turn the ad
on. Check it weekly. Members gained divided by money spent is your real cost
per member, and it is more honest than anything Meta would have told you.

Optimise for Landing Page Views, not conversions, since Meta has no conversion
to see.

## The creative

Middle of funnel means she already knows you. Do not introduce yourself. Give
her a reason to move today.

### Version A, the price

**Primary text**
> Nine dollars.
>
> That is less than one tray of lashes, and it is the whole Campus. The lash
> resource center, the business school, every Retention Room, and me in there
> answering questions.
>
> You have been watching from outside for a while now. Come inside.
>
> Nine dollars a month. Cancel any time, two clicks.

**Headline**
> Come Inside The Campus

**Description**
> $9/month · founding rate · cancel anytime

### Version B, the room

**Primary text**
> There is a room where women are figuring this out together.
>
> Some of them have been lashing for years. Some of them have not taken their
> first client yet. All of them stopped trying to work it out alone.
>
> That is the whole thing. Nine dollars a month.
>
> Come sit down.

**Headline**
> You Do Not Have To Figure It Out Alone

**Description**
> $9/month · founding rate · cancel anytime

**Run A and B in one ad set.** A sells the price, B sells the belonging. You do
not know which one your people move on.

### What to show

Whichever Retention Room post pulled hardest that week, re-cut to 10 seconds
with a Campus end card. Refresh it monthly so it does not go stale on a small
pool.

## Before you turn it on

**Fix the Skool about page.** The rewrite is in
`outreach/skool-about-page.md`. You would be sending paid traffic to a page
that promises how to make money and says you have served thousands of women.

**And get reviews on it.** Sixteen members, zero reviews. Paid traffic landing
on an empty star row is the most expensive kind of nothing. Four reviews before
you spend a dollar here.

---

## What good looks like

At $10 a day, $300 a month.

| | |
|---|---|
| Break even | 8 to 10 new members a month |
| Working | 12 or more |
| Not working | Under 6 for two months running, turn it off and reconsider |

Give it 60 days before you judge it. A holding pen does not pay out in 30.
