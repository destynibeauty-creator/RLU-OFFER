The research says retention and aftercare hooks book the most **for a lash

## The look, locked

Every prompt below assumes these. Paste them with each one or set them once in
your tool.

**Her.** Same woman in every shot. Black woman, late twenties, long straight
dark hair, full glam, long French tip nails. Composed, unhurried, direct to
camera when speaking.

**Wardrobe.** Crisp white lab coat worn open over an all black fitted outfit.
Gold jewellery. **Not scrubs.** Scrubs are what her two weakest posts wore. A
white coat in a lash feed is instantly readable as clinician and nobody else
in the category is wearing one.

**Environment.** The Lab. Near black, polished concrete, dark stone, brushed
gold trim, glass, city at dusk through floor to ceiling windows. Data screens
with pink readouts.

**Light.** Warm practical strips for shape, and one **hot pink #F600A2** rim or
practical in every frame. That pink glow is the signature. The category is
about to go purple with UV curing lamps. Pink is the differentiator and it is
already her colour.

**Palette line, on every prompt:** near black #0A0A0A ground, hot pink #F600A2
light, gold #D4AF37 accents, cool grey stone. No brown, no beige, no warm wood.

**Negative, on every prompt:** no text, no watermarks, no logos, no extra
fingers, no warped hands, no brown, no beige.

**Format.** 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920 minimum. Shoot 6 to 10 seconds a clip and
cut. Long AI clips drift.

# THE TALKING PIECES

## 1. The Lab open. Use once, day one.

> Cinematic 9:16 vertical. Slow forward dolly at chest height through a dark
> luxury laboratory studio. Polished concrete floor, dark stone surfaces,
> brushed gold trim, floor to ceiling glass with a city skyline at dusk. Wall
> mounted data screens glowing with pink readouts. A Black woman in her late
> twenties, long straight dark hair, full glam, wearing a crisp white lab coat
> open over an all black fitted outfit, stands with her back to camera looking
> at the screens, then turns her head toward the lens as the camera settles.
> Shallow depth of field, 35mm look, warm practical strip lighting with a hot
> pink rim light on her shoulders. Confident, still, expensive. 8 seconds.
> Palette: near black #0A0A0A, hot pink #F600A2, gold #D4AF37, cool grey stone.
> No brown, no beige, no warm wood. No text, no logos.

## 2. Three things destroying your retention. The workhorse.

This is the highest booking hook shape in the category. Shoot it three times
with three different framings and cut between them so it does not feel static.

> Cinematic 9:16 vertical. Medium close up, chest and head, of a Black woman in
> her late twenties with long straight dark hair and full glam, wearing a crisp
> white lab coat open over black, standing in a dark laboratory studio. She
> speaks directly to camera, calm and certain, small natural hand movements.
> Behind her, softly out of focus, a wall of dark data screens with pink
> readouts and a city skyline at dusk. 50mm look, shallow depth of field, eye
> level, camera locked with a very slight breathing drift. Key light soft from
> camera left, hot pink rim light on her hair and shoulder from behind right.
> 8 seconds. Palette: near black #0A0A0A, hot pink #F600A2, gold #D4AF37. No
> brown, no beige. No text, no logos.

**Alt framings for the cuts:** change *medium close up, chest and head* to
**tight close up, face filling frame, slow push in** for the second, and
**wide, full body, she walks slowly toward camera** for the third.

# THE MACROS

These are the pieces nobody in the category has. Shoot each one twice and keep
the better take.

## 4. The cure. In pink, while everyone else goes purple.

> Extreme macro photography, 9:16 vertical. A single droplet of clear
> cyanoacrylate adhesive at the join between two dark fibres, filling most of
> the frame. Visible moisture in the air drifting toward it and catching the
> light. The droplet surface hardening and crystallising from the outside
> inward over several seconds. Ultra shallow depth of field, clinical and still,
> slow motion. Lit from behind with a hot pink #F600A2 glow and a single gold
> specular highlight on the wet surface. Near black #0A0A0A background. Science
> documentary feel. 8 seconds. No brown, no beige, no purple. No text.

## 5. The nine days. The one that travels.

> Extreme macro photography, 9:16 vertical, time lapse. A clear hardened bond
> joining two dark fibres. A thin film of clear oil creeps slowly along the
> lower fibre and reaches the bond. The clear polymer begins to cloud, soften,
> and lose its grip. The upper fibre separates and slides away on its own,
> nothing touching it, nothing pulling. Ultra shallow depth of field, clinical,
> slow, inevitable. Hot pink #F600A2 rim light from behind, near black #0A0A0A
> background, one gold specular highlight. 10 seconds. No brown, no beige. No
> text.

## 6. The humidity fork.

> Extreme macro photography, 9:16 vertical, vertical split screen. Two
> identical clear adhesive droplets at identical fibre joins, one in each half.
> Top half: still, dry air, the droplet staying wet and glossy and never
> setting. Bottom half: visible moisture drifting in the air, the droplet
> setting cleanly to a matte finish. Both halves running at the same time.
> Clinical laboratory feel, ultra shallow depth of field. Hot pink #F600A2
> accent light, near black #0A0A0A, gold highlights. 8 seconds. No brown, no
> beige. No text.

## 7. Day one and day nine. Same set.

> Cinematic macro, 9:16 vertical. Extreme close up of a closed eye with a full
> set of lash extensions, dark skin, immaculate fan structure, every extension
> seated cleanly at the base. Hold for three seconds. Then a seamless time
> dissolve to the same eye nine days later: gaps in the line, several
> extensions twisted or hanging away from the lash, the base no longer clean.
> Nothing else in frame changes, same angle, same light. Ultra shallow depth of
> field, hot pink #F600A2 rim light along the lash line, near black background.
> 9 seconds. No brown, no beige. No text.

## 8. The lash cycle. Highest save rate format.

> Clean animated diagram, 9:16 vertical, on a near black #0A0A0A background. A
> single natural lash shown in cross section growing from the follicle, moving
> through growth, then resting, then shedding, then a new lash beginning. Thin
> gold #D4AF37 line work, hot pink #F600A2 highlight on whichever stage is
> active, soft glow. Elegant, minimal, medical illustration style, slow smooth
> motion. 10 seconds. No brown, no beige. No text, no labels.

Add your own labels afterwards in CapCut so the type matches the rest.

# ON SCREEN TEXT

Keep type in CapCut, not in the generation. AI text comes out warped and it
cost the studio render its product labels.

Same treatment every time so eight days read as one campaign:

- Poppins, 600 weight, white, centre, mid frame
- The one word that matters in **#F600A2**
- Never in the bottom third, the app puts its own UI there

**Hooks to run, in the shape the category rewards:**

> Three things destroying your retention right now
> Why do her lashes fall out in clumps a week after her fill?
> What is the real reason your sets never last past day ten?
> That is nine days in nine seconds
> Nobody pulled anything. It let go on its own.
> Your room decided this before she sat down.

Every one ends the same way: **Tuesday, Sept 1, 8 PM ET. Link in bio.**
