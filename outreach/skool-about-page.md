# Rich Lash® University Campus, Skool about page

This is where a woman lands at 8:58 PM on September 1 when Destyni says
"Campus members, stay on." It has to sell in about fifteen seconds.

## What was wrong

**1. An earnings promise.** "Learn lashes, how to get clients, what to post +
how to make money." The brand rule is no forward looking income or earnings
promise to a buyer, in any form. That is one.

**2. "Served thousands of women" overstates her own confirmed numbers.**
Confirmed is 1,000+ clients served and 250+ women trained. A thousand is not
thousands. The real numbers are more impressive than the vague one anyway.

**3. The Retention Room is not on the page.** This is the page the September 1
upsell points at and there is nothing on it about September 1, nothing about
staying until 9:45, nothing time bound at all.

**4. No reviews.** Sixteen paying members and an empty star row sitting where
social proof goes.

> ❌ No more guessing
> ❌ No more buying courses you never finish
> ❌ No more struggling to get clients
> ❌ No more figuring it all out alone
>
> ⚡ Join now and lock in the $9/month founding rate. Less than the cost of ONE

**Three edits, spelled out:**

"How to make money" became "how to run this like a business." Same promise of
competence, no promise of earnings.

"Served thousands of women" became the real numbers, which are better.

"Challenges + new trainings" became "Every Retention Room, live, plus the
replays", which is what the Campus actually is now.

**Take the September 1 block down on the 2nd** and replace it with the next
Room's date. A stale date on a sales page is worse than no date.
