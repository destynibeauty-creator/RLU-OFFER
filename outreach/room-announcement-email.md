## Send it plain

Do not rebuild the ticket email design for this. An announcement that looks
like a plain note from a person outperforms a designed campaign almost every
time, and you have eight days, not eight hours to spare on layout.

Klaviyo text block, left aligned, one button. That is the whole build.

## Two things before you hit send

**Turn Smart Sending off.** You have been testing all day. Smart Sending will
skip your own address and possibly others who got something in the last 16
hours.

**Do not exclude anybody.** Not the 199, not the graduates, not people who
already registered. A woman who already has a seat reading this again is
harmless. A woman who never hears about it is a lost registration.
