## About the three weeks line

**It is safe and it is your strongest asset.** It is a fact about your own
work, labelled as yours, in an invitation. It is not a promise about what the
reader will get, and it is not an income claim.

**What makes it safe is the line underneath it:**

> Yours are not going to last three weeks by Tuesday. That is not how any of
> this works.

That sentence does three things at once. It keeps the claim honest, it sounds
like you instead of a disclaimer, and it makes the promise you can actually
keep, which is that she will understand why.

**Never flip it into "come learn how to make your sets last three weeks."** You
cannot guarantee that from one hour, and a woman whose sets still shed in
September would be right to feel lied to. Same reason I flagged it on the
Reel.
